# Table artwork

`pirate-table.webp` was created with the built-in image generation tool, then encoded as WebP at quality 85 for local web delivery. It is bundled with the game and requires no external request. The reference screenshots informed the interface direction; the generated characters are new artwork.

Final generation prompt:

Dark weathered horizontal ship-deck wooden planks fill the whole image, warm brown charcoal palette, subtle worn old map engraving and brass flecks, strong dark vignette. An original charismatic female pirate duelist in a black tricorn with ivory lace edging, auburn flowing hair, white shirt and dark leather waistcoat holding a dagger occupies ONLY the lower LEFT corner, her bust rises from bottom edge and fills leftmost 25% and bottom 44%. An original sinister grinning male pirate captain with dark beard, black gold-trimmed tricorn, red cravat and a hook occupies ONLY upper RIGHT corner, cropped bust emerging from right edge filling rightmost 24% and top 42%. Illustrated painted characters with expressive faces, bold silhouette, golden highlights, detailed antique adventure game aesthetic. Keep middle 50% across the entire image EMPTY dark wooden planks suitable for readable live game UI. A few piles of antique gold coins at upper left and lower right extreme corners. NO letters, no text, no numbers, no UI, no buttons, no cards, no borders. This is a production background art asset; do not draw a device or screenshot.
