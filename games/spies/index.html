    How to play

    On your turn, play two different agents: one face-up and one face-down. After that, draw back up to four cards. Your rival chooses one of the two agents; you recruit the other.

    When you recruit an agent, count how many of that type you have in play. Use the first, second, or third icon on its card to move your meeple clockwise. A negative number moves backward.

    At the end of each turn, you win by catching Kestrel or by having three Codebreakers. You lose with three Daredevils. If both sides qualify at once, the active player wins.

    Optional discards are available up to four times per game, before you play your two agents.
