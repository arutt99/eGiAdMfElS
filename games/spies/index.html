    How to play

    The player who watched a spy movie most recently starts.

    1. Play. Offer two different agent names: one face-up, one face-down. If your entire hand has one name, a matching pair is allowed. Then draw back up to four cards.
    2. Recruit. Your rival takes one offered card; you take the other. Reveal the hidden card and add both to the appropriate tableau.
    3. Move. Count that agent type in your tableau and resolve its first, second, or third icon. Fourth and later copies still use the third icon. Negative values move counterclockwise.
    4. End. After both meeples finish moving, check every win and loss condition.
    WinCatch the rival by reaching or passing them, or recruit three Codebreakers.
    LoseRecruit three Daredevils.

    You may discard and replace cards before playing, up to four times per game. You may do this repeatedly in one turn while the deck has cards.

    If the deck is empty, continue without drawing. When the next player has fewer than two cards, the closer player wins. Any tied end condition goes to the active player.
