    Game menu

    1. Draw. On your turn, tap the deck or Draw. You must draw at least one card.
    2. Mind the danger line. Once three or more cards are in front of you, drawing a value you already have is a bust: every exposed card goes to the Lost pile.
    3. Steal. Draw a value someone else has exposed and you may take all of their cards of that value.
    4. Keep. Stop whenever you like. Your cards stay exposed, and can be stolen, until your next turn starts. Then they bank into your Loot for good.
    5. Win. When the deck runs out, everyone banks what's in front of them. The richest Loot wins.

    The deck has 90 cards: eleven each of 1 to 5 and seven each of 6 to 10.
