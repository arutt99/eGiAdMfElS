How to play
  • Tap the deck to draw at least one card on your turn.
  • Three exposed cards are the danger line: matching a value busts your turn.
  • Matching exposed cards can be stolen before you keep the new card.
  • Keep your cards exposed to press on, or bank them safely on your next turn.
