    Avoid the tricks

    Each trick, play one card. If you hold a card in any suit already in the trick, you must play one of those suits. Otherwise, play any card.

    The suit with the most cards wins the trick. If suits tie, the highest card among those tied suits wins. The player who wins normally leads the next trick.

    If the won trick contains the highest card of any suit, its winner may choose the next leader. A round ends when every hand is empty; every trick taken adds one penalty point.
