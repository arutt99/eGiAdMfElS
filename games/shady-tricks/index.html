      Game menu

      1. The deck. Four colours of −3 to 9 (no 0), plus two Jesters worth 0 and 10. Everyone gets 12 cards; two are set aside unseen.
      2. Follow the lead. The first coloured card played sets the lead colour. Follow it if you can; if you can't, play anything. A Jester can be played at any time and never sets the lead.
      3. Second best takes it. Of the lead-colour cards and Jesters, the second-highest takes the trick. If only one is there, it takes it.
      4. What the taker gets. Their own card, plus every off-colour card in the trick. The other lead-colour cards and Jesters are thrown away. The taker leads next.
      5. Pairs cancel. A card with the same number as one already in your tableau cancels it, and both leave. Use it to dump a big card, or to rescue a negative one.
      6. Scoring. After 12 tricks, your tableau is added to your total (it never drops below 0). The lowest unique total leads the next round.
      7. Don't fall. When anyone ends a round over 40, the game ends. The highest total at or below 40 wins. Leaders who tie knock each other out.

      Example

      Red is led. Red 8, red 5 and a blue 3 are played. The red 5 is second-highest, so it takes the trick with the blue 3: +8. The red 8 is thrown away.
